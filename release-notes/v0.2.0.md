The tracks gtube expects to play next start in about a tenth of a second instead of 4 to 7, and the visualizer gains two new effects drawn in [Bend](https://github.com/bendlang/bend): a campfire, and a flight over land made of voxels that the music reshapes. Bend effects now open in about half a second, and gtube builds and signs in on macOS from source.

gtube keeps its data in a new folder, `%APPDATA%\wakamex\gtube` on Windows and `~/.local/share/wakamex/gtube` on Linux. At first launch it moves the old `gesso-gtube` folder there, with your session and queue.

## Highlights

- Next tracks, a restored queue, a song left selected and the first liked song start in about 0.1 s, from addresses [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) fetched ahead. On Windows yt-dlp itself starts in 0.4 s instead of 1.1 s.
- With nothing queued, gtube opens on your liked music.
- Bend voxels: a flight over land built of square columns, drawn the way NovaLogic's [Comanche: Maximum Overkill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comanche:_Maximum_Overkill) drew its land in 1992. The bass heaves the hills, the mids roll ripples across them and each beat sends a ring of rising columns outward.
- Bend flames: a campfire in a ring of stones, seen from a slowly circling camera, its separate tongues of fire lighting the logs and ground.
- Fire is Doom's fire, with tongues instead of a red haze.
- Bend effects open in about half a second: gtube has the GPU driver prepare their program in the background at launch, which used to take 4 to 8 s the first time an effect was shown.
- The visualizer holds still while playback is paused, and typing no longer blanks the screen on the Vulkan renderer.
- On Windows, requests to YouTube go through WinHTTP with reused connections instead of a `curl.exe` per request.
- The window title is just gtube.
